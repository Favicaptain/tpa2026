WHO WE ARE

About the Organisation

Tinubu Progressive Advocates is a national grassroots movement committed to advancing progressive leadership, empowering young Nigerians, and strengthening the bonds that unite us as one people.

Through sport, civic education and community mobilisation, we work state by state, ward by ward, to ensure every Nigerian has a seat at the table and a voice in shaping our shared future.
